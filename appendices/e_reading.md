# Appendix E. Further Reading and Free Simulations

Books, courses and free interactive simulations by others that go well with this tutorial.
{: .chapterlead }

## Books

- L. Chrostowski and M. Hochberg, *Silicon Photonics Design: From Devices to Systems* {% include cite.html keys="chrostowski2015" %}: the design-oriented textbook that most of Parts II–IV and VII follow in spirit.
- B. E. A. Saleh and M. C. Teich, *Fundamentals of Photonics* {% include cite.html keys="salehteich" %}: electromagnetic optics, beams, fibres and lasers.
- K. Okamoto, *Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides* {% include cite.html keys="okamoto" %}: waveguide theory, couplers, AWGs.
- L. A. Coldren, S. W. Corzine and M. L. Mašanović, *Diode Lasers and Photonic Integrated Circuits* {% include cite.html keys="coldren" %}: the reference for Part VI.

## Courses

- [UBCx Phot1x: Silicon Photonics Design, Fabrication and Data Analysis](https://www.edx.org/learn/engineering/university-of-british-columbia-silicon-photonics-design-fabrication-and-data-ana) on edX, with real fabrication through openEBL {% include cite.html keys="phot1x" %}.
- [AIM Photonics Academy online courses](https://www.aimphotonics.com/online-courses): fundamentals, passive and active device testing, detectors and modulators, sensors, and fabless design {% include cite.html keys="aimacademy" %}.
