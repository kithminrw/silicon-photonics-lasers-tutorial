# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 19</span>Electro-Optic Effects Across Platforms

To modulate at tens of gigahertz you need a faster knob than heat, and each platform offers a different one.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- use the Soref–Bennett relations for plasma dispersion in silicon;
- compare carrier injection, depletion and accumulation;
- describe the Pockels effect in LN and BTO, including crystal orientation and field overlap;
- describe electro-absorption (Franz–Keldysh, QCSE);
- compare platforms by VπL, loss and bandwidth.

</div>

<div class="tbox tbox-insight" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">Design question</div>

Which effect gives the best VπL for a given loss and bandwidth?

</div>
