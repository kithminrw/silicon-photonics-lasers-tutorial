# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 27</span>Lasers on Silicon

Silicon cannot lase efficiently, so the light source is brought in: bonded, printed, flip-chipped or grown.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- compare off-chip, flip-chip, heterogeneous, micro-transfer-printed and monolithic lasers;
- explain adiabatic III–V/Si mode transfer;
- estimate self-heating and thermal impedance;
- describe the open laser-integration projects (SiEPICfab Shuksan, UBC ELEC 413).

</div>

Self-heating, thermal impedance and ageing.
