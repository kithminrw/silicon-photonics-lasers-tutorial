# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 21</span>Photodetectors and Receivers

At the end of every link a detector turns photons back into electrons; its responsivity, dark current and bandwidth set the receiver's sensitivity.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- compute responsivity from quantum efficiency;
- describe vertical and lateral Ge-on-Si photodiodes coupled to waveguides;
- find the bandwidth from RC and transit-time limits;
- include dark current, noise and avalanche gain;
- compute receiver sensitivity for a target bit-error ratio.

</div>
