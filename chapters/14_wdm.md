# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 14</span>WDM Filters and Polarisation Management

Wavelength-division multiplexing puts many channels on one waveguide, and polarisation devices deal with fibres that scramble polarisation.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- name the WDM grids (CWDM, LAN-WDM, DWDM) and their requirements;
- compare mux/demux architectures: ring rows, MZI lattices, AWGs, echelles and contra-DCs;
- estimate crosstalk and insertion loss;
- explain polarisation splitters and rotators and polarisation-diversity circuits.

</div>
