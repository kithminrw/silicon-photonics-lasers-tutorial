# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 13</span>Bragg Gratings and Photonic Crystals

A periodic change of index reflects one band of wavelengths and passes the rest: the integrated version of a dielectric mirror.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- state the Bragg condition and relate the stop-band to the coupling coefficient κ;
- compute reflectivity and bandwidth with coupled-mode theory and with transfer matrices;
- apodise a grating to suppress side lobes;
- build cavities from phase-shifted gratings;
- describe contra-directional couplers, SWG metamaterials and photonic crystals.

</div>

<div class="tbox tbox-insight" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">Design question</div>

How long and how strong must a grating be for a given reflectivity and bandwidth?

</div>
