# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 9</span>Coupled-Mode Theory and Directional Couplers

Bring two waveguides close together and light moves back and forth between them; controlling that exchange is the basis of most passive devices.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- derive the coupled-mode equations for two waveguides;
- explain coupling with even and odd supermodes and the beat length;
- find the length for any power split, and its dependence on gap, wavelength and polarisation;
- describe asymmetric, adiabatic and broadband couplers;
- write the 2 × 2 scattering matrix and explain its −j.

</div>
