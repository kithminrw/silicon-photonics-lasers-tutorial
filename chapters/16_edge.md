# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 16</span>Edge Couplers and Spot-Size Converters

Coupling at the chip edge is broadband and low-loss, at the price of a prepared facet and tight alignment.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- explain how an inverse taper expands the mode;
- describe multi-stage, SiN, polymer and suspended couplers;
- compute overlap with cleaved and lensed fibres;
- handle facet reflections with angled waveguides and coatings;
- compare edge and grating couplers.

</div>
