# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 24</span>The Fabry–Pérot Laser and the Rate Equations

Put gain between two mirrors, pump it until gain equals loss, and the device lases.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- write the threshold condition from the round trip;
- compute the longitudinal-mode spacing and photon lifetime;
- solve the carrier and photon rate equations for the L–I curve;
- extract threshold current, slope efficiency and differential quantum efficiency;
- explain the temperature dependence (T₀), turn-on delay and relaxation oscillations.

</div>
