# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 8</span>Bends, Tapers and Transitions

Chips route light around corners and between widths and layers, and every transition is a chance to lose it.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- explain bend loss: radiation and mode mismatch, TE against TM;
- design Euler and Bézier bends;
- design adiabatic tapers;
- compute junction loss from mode overlap;
- describe crossings and transitions between layers (Si to SiN).

</div>

<div class="tbox tbox-insight" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">Design question</div>

How tight can a bend be, and how short a taper?

</div>

## <span class="secnum">8.1</span>Radiation loss in bends

The conformal-transformation picture, the mode shifting outward, minimum radius per platform.

## <span class="secnum">8.2</span>Mode mismatch at straight–bend junctions

Offsets, Euler (clothoid) and Bézier bends.

## <span class="secnum">8.3</span>Tapers and adiabaticity

Linear, parabolic and exponential tapers and the adiabatic length criterion.

## <span class="secnum">8.4</span>Overlap integrals and butt coupling

Junction loss between different waveguides.

## <span class="secnum">8.5</span>Waveguide crossings

MMI-based crossings and crosstalk.

## <span class="secnum">8.6</span>Transitions between layers

Si-to-SiN escalators and mode converters.
