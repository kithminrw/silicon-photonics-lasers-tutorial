# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 20</span>Modulators

A modulator writes data onto light; its speed, swing and loss set the reach and energy of an optical link.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- define modulator metrics: extinction ratio, OMA, insertion loss, bandwidth, energy per bit;
- design lumped and travelling-wave MZMs (velocity and impedance matching);
- design ring modulators and explain their thermal control;
- compare EAMs and TFLN and BTO modulators;
- read NRZ, PAM4 and coherent eye and constellation diagrams.

</div>
