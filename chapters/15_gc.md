# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 15</span>Grating Couplers

A grating etched into the waveguide diffracts light up towards a fibre, so a chip can be tested anywhere on the wafer.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- write the phase-matching condition and draw its wavevector diagram;
- relate period, angle and wavelength;
- explain directionality and the role of the buried-oxide thickness;
- estimate coupling efficiency from mode overlap, bandwidth and back-reflection;
- describe apodised, focusing, SWG and 2D grating couplers.

</div>
