# <span class="chapnum">Chapter 18</span>Thermo-Optic Phase Shifters

Heating silicon changes its index enough to tune any device, at a cost in power and speed.
{: .chapterlead }

<p><span class="status status-outline">outline</span> This chapter is an outline: the sections, demos and reading are planned, and the text is still to be written.</p>

<div class="tbox tbox-objectives" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">In this chapter you will learn to</div>

- compare thermo-optic coefficients (Si ≈ 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ /K, SiN and SiO₂ about ten times smaller);
- derive Pπ and the thermal time constant from a thermal circuit;
- compare metal and doped-silicon heaters;
- improve efficiency with trenches, undercuts and folded paths;
- handle thermal crosstalk and lock a ring to a laser.

</div>

<div class="tbox tbox-insight" markdown="1">
<div class="tbox-title">Design question</div>

How many milliwatts for a π shift, and how fast?

</div>
