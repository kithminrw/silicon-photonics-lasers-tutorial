Three things to try

  1. Widen the silicon slowly. Up to about 0.6 µm the mode stays in the III–V (4 % in the silicon). Between 0.75 and 1.0 µm it moves down: 0.85 µm shares it 37 / 38 %.
  2. Finish the taper. At 1.5 µm only 14 % is left in the MQW region and 78 % is in the silicon: the light has left the gain section, so the III–V mesa can end in a tip.
  3. Look at the partner. At 0.85 µm switch to the partner: its index is lower, 3.162 against 3.218, and it splits the light differently, 19.6 % in the MQW and 43.5 % in the silicon. Its field in the silicon is blue, opposite in sign to the red above it: the antiphase supermode.
