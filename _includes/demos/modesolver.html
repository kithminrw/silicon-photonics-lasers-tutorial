Three things to try

  1. Check it against femwell. The default 500 × 220 nm strip gives neff = 2.4468 and ng = 4.181 (Table 6.1: 2.446 and 4.18); the effective-index method gave 2.492. Press Ex: the field jumps up just outside the side walls, where it is normal to them. Press Ez: the longitudinal field reaches 51 % of the peak.
  2. Switch to TM-like. neff falls to 1.7731 and ng to 3.753 (Table 6.1: 1.771 and 3.74), and only 44 % of the power stays in the silicon: Ey jumps by (nSi/nox)² = 5.8 at the top and bottom faces, so much of the mode sits in the oxide above and below the core.
  3. Find the single-mode width. Back on TE-like, press first higher and narrow W: TE1 is still guided at 440 nm (neff = 1.4462, just above the oxide's 1.444) and cut off at 430 nm, close to the 450 nm of Figure 6.1. Then press Si rib: neff = 2.5684 (Table 6.1: 2.568), and the mode spreads into the slab.
