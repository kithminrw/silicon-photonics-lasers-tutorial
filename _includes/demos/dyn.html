Three things to try

  1. Raise the bias. From 50 to 120 mA, fr grows from 4.7 to 8.4 GHz and the bandwidth from 7.2 to 12.6 GHz, as √(I − Ith); the response peak flattens as the damping grows.
  2. Shorten the cavity. At 50 mA, go from 400 to 200 µm: fr rises to 5.6 GHz and the bandwidth to 8.4 GHz, but the linewidth almost triples, to 5.9 MHz, because the photons leave sooner.
  3. Switch off the α-factor. Set α = 0: the linewidth falls from 2.08 to 0.12 MHz, the Schawlow–Townes value, seventeen times narrower (1 + α² = 17).
