Three things to try

  1. Offset a large mode. On fibre to grating, slide the offset to 2.4 µm: the coupling falls by about 1 dB, as Worked Example 17.1 predicts, and the beam lands off the chip mode.
  2. Tilt both cases. Back at zero offset, tilt the grating case by 3°: −1.3 dB, and the crests meet the chip’s at an angle. On lensed fibre to taper the same 3° costs only 0.08 dB, but 0.6 µm of offset costs 1 dB.
  3. Open a gap with small spots. On lensed fibre to taper, set the gap to 3 µm: the beam spreads within a few micrometres and the coupling falls 0.9 dB. Choose index-matched and the loss halves, to 0.44 dB.
