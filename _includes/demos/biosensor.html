Three things to try

  1. Switch to TM. The same 5 × 10⁻⁴ RIU change moves the resonance by 103 pm instead of 27 pm. In the side view the field reaches about twice as far into the water, and its strongest part sits just outside the silicon.
  2. Sharpen the resonance. Raise the Q from 20 000 to 100 000: the dip narrows from 77.7 to 15.5 pm, so even the TE shift is now well clear of the linewidth.
  3. Find the limit. With TE and 1 pm of tracking noise, lower the index change until the pill reads IN THE NOISE: below 5.6 × 10⁻⁵ RIU the shift is under 3σ.
