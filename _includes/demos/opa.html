Three things to try

  1. Lose the grating lobes. At the default 2 µm pitch the main beam at 7.4° comes with two grating lobes, and the near field is a criss-cross of wavefronts. Close the pitch to 0.75 µm: one clean set of tilted wavefronts is left, now at 20.1°.
  2. Steer. At 0.75 µm, raise the phase step from 60° to 120°: the beam swings from 20.1° to 43.5° and broadens as 1/cos θ, from 6.98° to 9.05°.
  3. Narrow the beam. Back at 2 µm and 60°, go from 16 to 64 emitters: the 128 µm aperture makes the beam four times narrower, 0.62° instead of 2.48°.
