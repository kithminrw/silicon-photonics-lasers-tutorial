Three things to try

  1. Follow one crest. At the defaults, a silicon wire (n = 2.44, ng = 4.20), watch the white dot: it is born at the tail of the packet and dies at its head, because vp = 0.410c beats vg = 0.238c.
  2. Remove the dispersion. Set ng = n = 2.44: the crests ride with the envelope, and the delay of 1 cm falls from 140 ps to 81 ps.
  3. Spread the packet. Raise the group-velocity dispersion to 0.5 and press restart: the packet widens and its peak drops as it travels, as a pulse does over kilometres of fibre (Worked Example 2.5).
