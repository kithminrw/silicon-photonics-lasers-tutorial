Three things to try

  1. Tune the BOX. Move it from 2 µm to 1.89 µm: the directionality rises from 62 % to 71 % as the substrate reflection returns in phase and the upward field brightens. At 2.16 µm it returns out of phase and only 29 % goes up.
  2. Weaken the grating. Set α to 0.04 µm⁻¹ and the fibre to 8 µm. The emission now spreads past the fibre mode, 20 % of the light leaves the end of the grating unused, and the efficiency falls to 34 % (Table 15.1).
  3. Apodise and add the mirror. Choose apodised and bottom mirror, then set α to its maximum, the fibre to 8 µm and the BOX to 1.89 µm. The emitted field now follows the fibre’s Gaussian: 99 % overlap and 94 % (−0.3 dB) in all.
