Three things to try

  1. Offset both interfaces. With the FaML pair, slide the offset to 6 µm: the coupling falls by about 1 dB, as Worked Example 17.2 predicts. Switch to the lensed fibre: the same 6 µm sends it far off the bottom of the plot, which already reached −1 dB at 0.6 µm.
  2. Find the price. Back at zero offset, tilt the FaML pair by 1.1°: about −1 dB. The lensed fibre loses only 0.01 dB at that angle. A wide beam has a narrow far field, so the parts must be held parallel, which flat reference faces and mechanical stops do well.
  3. Grow the beam. Set the expanded beam to 50 µm: the 1 dB offset doubles to ±12 µm and the tilt tolerance halves to 0.54°, but the lens on the fibre grows to 394 µm. Push past 79 µm and the lens bodies no longer fit side by side on a 127 µm array.
