Three things to try

  1. Cross the critical angle. With silicon over oxide (3.48 / 1.44), sweep θ1 from 20° to 30°. Past θc = 24.4° the transmitted wave becomes an evanescent one that only runs along the interface, and the pill reads TOTAL INTERNAL REFLECTION.
  2. Squeeze the tail. Still in total internal reflection, go from 25° to 55°: the decay length falls from 825 nm to 100 nm. At 55°, the ray angle of the 220 nm silicon slab, it is the 100 nm of Worked Example 2.4.
  3. Find Brewster's angle. Switch to p and set θ1 = 22.5°. R drops to zero and the field above the interface is the incident wave alone, with no ripple from a reflection.
