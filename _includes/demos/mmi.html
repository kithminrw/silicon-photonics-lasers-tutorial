Three things to try

  1. Split 50/50. Press 1 × 2: L = 3Lπ/8 = 33.7 µm puts two images of the centred input on the output guides at ±W/4, and the 50/50 pill lights up. The crests in the two outputs are in step.
  2. Offset the input. Set the offset to 1.5 µm (W/4) and press 2 × 2: two images again, 50/50, but now a quarter period apart, as in a 3 dB coupler. Press single image: the mirrored image leaves by the lower guide.
  3. Double the width. At W = 12 µm, Lπ grows four-fold to 356 µm and the 1 × 2 splitter needs 133.5 µm: wide MMIs are long.
