Three things to try

  1. Remove the thickness variation. Set its range to 0: σ halves, from 2.26 to 1.11 nm, and the heater budget falls from 49 to 23 mW. Thickness counts 1.4 nm per nanometre, width 0.6.
  2. Leave only the random part. Set both wafer-scale ranges to 0: 72 % of the dies land within ±1 nm, but the full range is still 4.0 nm, and the bluest die still needs 22 mW.
  3. Make new wafers. At the default ranges, press new wafer a few times: the heater budget swings between about 44 and 58 mW. Budget for the worst wafer, not the average.
