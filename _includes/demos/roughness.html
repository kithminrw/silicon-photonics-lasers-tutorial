Three things to try

  1. Widen the strip. Set W to 1, 2 and 3 µm. |Es|² falls from 0.548 µm⁻¹ to 0.114, 0.0187 and 0.00608, and the sidewall loss from 1.76 dB/cm to 0.358, 0.0582 and 0.0189 dB/cm: 0.203, 0.0330 and 0.0107 of the 500 nm strip’s, the numbers of Figure 7.2.
  2. Smooth the walls. At 500 nm, set σ to 0.8 nm: the loss falls from 1.76 to 0.501 dB/cm, (0.8/1.5)² = 0.28 of it. Then set Lc to 20 and to 100 nm: 0.246 and 0.655 dB/cm, yet at W = 2 µm the ratio to the 500 nm strip stays between 0.0316 and 0.0338.
  3. Take the top oxide away. Press air clad: the larger index step at the sidewalls raises the 500 nm strip’s loss from 1.76 to 2.24 dB/cm. Back in oxide, set λ to 1310 nm: the loss rises to 2.47 dB/cm, because shorter waves scatter more.
