Three things to try

  1. Check it against the full solver. The default 500 × 220 nm strip gives neff = 2.492 and single-mode below 322 nm; femwell gives 2.446 and about 450 nm. Narrow W to 300 nm and the pill reads SINGLE-MODE.
  2. Etch a rib. Press Si rib 220/90: nII rises from 1.444 to 2.104, the lateral contrast falls, and the single-mode width grows from 322 to 406 nm. neff = 2.600, against 2.568 from femwell (Table 6.1).
  3. Switch to TM-like. On the strip, neff falls to 1.847 (femwell: 1.771). Its vertical slab index nI is only 2.054, so the guide stays single-mode up to 533 nm.
