Three things to try

  1. Meet the target. Set the junction efficiency to 50 pm/V and the detuning to −109 pm: 4.3 dB of extinction at 3.0 dB of loss. The target is met, but slowly: c/(λQ) is only 24 GHz.
  2. Watch the ring fill. At the defaults, flip the field between low and high bias. At low bias the resonance sits nearer the laser: the ring holds 15 times the bus power and only 28 % gets through; at high bias, 7 times and 68 %.
  3. Trade Q for speed. Lower the loaded Q to 5000: the bandwidth rises to 39 GHz, but the dip widens, and even at 50 pm/V and −109 pm the loss reaches 4.0 dB.
