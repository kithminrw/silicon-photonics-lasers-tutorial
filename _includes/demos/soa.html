Three things to try

  1. Saturate it. Raise the input from −15 to −1.3 dBm: the gain falls by 3 dB, to 10.0 dB, with 8.7 dBm out, as in Worked Example 28.1. Towards the output the crests grow more slowly: the gain is saturating there.
  2. Make it longer. Back at −15 dBm, double the length to 2 mm: the small-signal gain doubles in decibels, to 26.1 dB, but the −3 dB output power stays near 8.3 dBm.
  3. Raise the saturation power. At 1 mm, go from 12 to 24 mW: the −3 dB output power rises by 3 dB, to 11.7 dBm, while the small-signal gain stays at 13.0 dB.
