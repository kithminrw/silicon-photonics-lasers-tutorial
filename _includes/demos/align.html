Three things to try

  1. Search blind. Press new chip and step with the arrows. Until the array is within about 13 µm of the couplers the meter reads only noise, and no direction looks better than another.
  2. Let the machine do it. Press auto: a spiral with a 3 µm pitch, finer than the 10 µm mode, finds first light, then hill-climbing in halving steps reaches the peak, usually in 10 to 35 moves.
  3. Correct the yaw. Each chip sits at a small random yaw. Once aligned, move the yaw slider in small steps and re-peak: every degree of error moves the second fibre, 127 µm away, 2.2 µm off its coupler, about 0.9 dB.
