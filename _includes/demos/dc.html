Three things to try

  1. Make a 3 dB coupler. At a 200 nm gap, set L to 16.4 µm: Lx/2 = 18.9 µm, less the 2.5 µm the bends add. The 50/50 pill lights up. All along the coupler the crests in the lower guide trail a quarter period behind those in the upper one: that is the −j of Section 9.6.
  2. Open the gap to 300 nm. Lx grows to about 91 µm, so the same 16.4 µm now sends only about 10 % across. Find the new 50/50 length; Worked Example 9.1 gets 43 µm.
  3. Go all the way across and back. At 200 nm, set L + 2.5 µm equal to Lx (L = 35.3 µm): all the light leaves in the lower guide. Make it twice as long (L = 73.1 µm) and the light is back where it started.
