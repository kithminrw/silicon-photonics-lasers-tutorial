Three things to try

  1. Detune the period. Set Λ to 630 nm. The centre moves to 1595 nm, and at 1550 nm the beam now leaves at 13.5° in air, well off the 8° fibre: the pill reads mismatched.
  2. Stand the fibre up. Set θ to 0°: the peak jumps to 1615 nm. Bring it back with Λ = 576 nm. The beam now goes straight up, but that period also meets the second-order Bragg condition, so the grating reflects light back into the waveguide.
  3. Take the oxide off. Choose air clad. The centre wavelength stays at 1550 nm, because only the angle in air enters the phase matching, but the beam now leaves the grating at 8.0° instead of 5.5°, with its crests further apart.
