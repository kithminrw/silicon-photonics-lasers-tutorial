Three things to try

  1. Change the doping. At 1.0 × 10¹⁸ cm⁻³ VπL improves to 1.11 V·cm, but the loss more than doubles, to 24.8 dB/cm. At 2.0 × 10¹⁷ it is 2.05 V·cm for only 3.4 dB/cm, as in Table 19.1.
  2. Turn up the bias. At the default doping, 1 V gives 1.24 V·cm, 2 V 1.42 and 4 V 1.73: the depletion region widens only as √(Vbi + V). The capacitance falls as it widens, from 0.23 to 0.14 fF/µm.
  3. Reach a π shift. Lengthen the phase shifter to 6 mm and raise the bias to about 2.6 V: the phase passes π, for 6.4 dB of loss.
