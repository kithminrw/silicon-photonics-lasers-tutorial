Three things to try

  1. Use the measured couplers. Set the grating-coupler loss to 4 dB each, the PDK design: the received power falls to −4.7 dBm and the margin to 3.9 dB.
  2. Spend the margin on power. Back at 3 dB, lower the laser to +7.5 dBm: 3.4 dB of margin is left, and the energy falls from 3.65 to 3.07 pJ/bit.
  3. Use a poor laser. Drop the wall-plug efficiency to 5 %: the lasers alone cost 4.0 pJ/bit, the total reaches 6.32 pJ/bit, and the energy target fails although the budget still closes.
