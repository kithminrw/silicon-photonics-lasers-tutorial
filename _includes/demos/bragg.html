Three things to try

  1. Probe the stop band. At λB the forward wave fades along the grating and the backward wave grows towards the input, where the two beat into a standing wave. Slide the probe past the first null: the envelopes stop decaying and most of the light gets through.
  2. Reach 99 %. Keep κ = 12.6 /mm and raise N to 750 periods: κL = 3.0 and the peak reflectivity reaches 99 %, as in Worked Example 13.2. Almost nothing reaches the far end.
  3. Open a cavity. Press λ/4 shift with the probe at λB: the light gets through again, and the field piles up in the middle of the grating, between its two mirrors.
