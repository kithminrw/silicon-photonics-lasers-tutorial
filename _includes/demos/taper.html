Three things to try

  1. Match a lensed fibre. Choose the lensed fibre and set the tip to 180 nm: the mode is 2.9 × 2.8 µm and couples 92 % (−0.36 dB), with under 1 % of it below 2 µm.
  2. Go for SMF-28. Choose SMF-28 and narrow the tip to 140 nm. The overlap reaches 89 %, but 14 % of the mode now lies more than 2 µm below the core: on a 2 µm BOX that light leaks into the substrate.
  3. Switch to TM. At the same 160 nm tip the TM mode is only 2.2 µm and the UHNA coupling falls to 71 %. TM reaches the 4 µm spot at a narrower tip: try 120 nm (90 %).
