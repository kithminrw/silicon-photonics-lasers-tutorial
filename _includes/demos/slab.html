Three things to try

  1. Find the single-mode limit. With Si / SiO₂ at 1550 nm, thicken the film from 220 nm. At 250 nm TE1 appears (V = π at 245 nm, Worked Example 5.2): the pill turns to MULTIMODE and a second, steeper ray joins the side view.
  2. Switch to TM. At 220 nm, neff falls from 2.848 to 2.053 (Worked Example 5.1). The crests, λ/neff apart, spread out and the field reaches further into the oxide.
  3. Put air on top. Set n3 = 1.00, stay on TM and thin the film: below about 105 nm the pill reads NOT GUIDED. An asymmetric slab cuts off even its fundamental mode.
