Three things to try

  1. Find the cut-off. On standard SMF, slide λ below 1318 nm: V passes 2.405, LP11 joins LP01 and the pill changes to multimode.
  2. Shrink the core. Set a = 2.5 µm. V falls to 1.25 and the MFD grows to 12.9 µm, larger than with the 4.1 µm core: the weakly guided mode spreads into the cladding.
  3. Raise the NA. Choose the high-NA fibre (a = 1.25 µm, NA 0.35). It is still single-mode, with a 3.6 µm MFD, close to the spots of on-chip edge couplers.
