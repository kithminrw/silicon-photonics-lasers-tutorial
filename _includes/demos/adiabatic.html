Three things to try

  1. Check the PDK tapers. The default is the EBeam PDK’s 20 µm linear taper from 500 nm to 3 µm: worst-case margin 1.43, at its wide end, and 0.60 % of the power into TE2 (the eigenmode expansion, the circle at 20 µm, gives 0.69 %). At L = 40 µm the margin doubles to 2.86 and TE2 falls to 0.16 %.
  2. Shape the taper. Press constant margin and set L to 12.0 µm: the margin is 1.43 all along, as for the 20 µm linear taper. The two-mode estimate reads 0.23 %, at a dip of its curve, but the expansion’s circle shows 0.61 %, about the linear taper’s 0.69 %: the same margin and about the same conversion in 60 % of the length.
  3. Make it too short. Back to linear, set L to 5 µm: the margin falls to 0.36, the pill reads CRITERION MISSED and 6.1 % goes into TE2 (the circles at 4 and 6 µm show 12 % and 7.2 %). In the top view the crests bend where TE2 beats with TE0.
