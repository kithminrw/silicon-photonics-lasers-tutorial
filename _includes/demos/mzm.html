Three things to try

  1. See the drive in the arms. At the defaults (silicon, 3 mm, 2 Vpp, quadrature), flip the field between low and high drive: the arms' crests slip from 51° to 129° apart, and the output falls from 38 % to 9 % of the input.
  2. Lengthen the silicon modulator to 6 mm. Vπ halves to 2.33 V, but the loss grows to 9.7 dB and the bandwidth falls to 8 GHz: each bit has no time to settle, and the eye starts to close.
  3. Switch to thin-film LiNbO₃ at 10 mm. Vπ is 2.60 V, the bandwidth 100 GHz and the loss 3.3 dB, almost all of it from sitting at quadrature: the eye opens wide, with 14.7 dB of extinction.
