Three things to try

  1. Meet the −20 dB target. Lower κ² to 13.0 %: the passband narrows to 0.57 nm (QL 2710), the worst crosstalk reaches −20.1 dB and its pill turns green, while the drop losses hardly move (0.06 to 0.17 dB).
  2. Switch the heaters off. At 20 °C every ring sits 2 nm blue of its channel, and each photodiode gets its own channel 15.7 to 16.0 dB down. Warm the chip: at 47 °C the drift (74 pm/K over 27 K) has carried every ring onto its channel; at 70 °C they are 1.7 nm red, and the drop loss is back up to 14.5 to 14.6 dB.
  3. Lock at 70 °C. Locked to their own channels, the rings must be heated almost a whole FSR to their next orders: 45 mW each, 180 mW in all. Locked to the nearest channels they need 6.1 mW each: ring 1 now drops λ2, ring 2 λ3, ring 3 λ4, and ring 4 drops λ1 through its next order. Pick λ1 to watch its light pass three rings and leave at the last.
