Three things to try

  1. Mismatch the garnet. On the MZI, lower the non-reciprocal phase from 45° to 30° per arm: the isolation falls from beyond 40 dB to about 11 dB, and 0.3 dB of the forward light is lost. The reciprocal −π/2 was designed for 45°; the two must match.
  2. Trade bandwidth for delay. Raise the delay order from 1 to 20 and widen your view with the probe: the 20 dB window shrinks from 76 nm to about 3 nm. Then add 1 dB of extra loss in one arm: even at the design point, unequal arms cannot cancel completely, and the isolation is capped near 25 dB.
  3. Try a ring. Switch to the ring: the non-reciprocal phase splits the resonance, so at the probe the backward light sits on its resonance and is absorbed while the forward light is off resonance. Reverse the magnet: the roles swap, and the device now isolates the other way.
