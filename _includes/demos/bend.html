Three things to try

  1. Count the cost. At the default R = 5 µm, the full Euler bend's tightest radius is 2.68 µm against 5.0 µm, and it is 8.41 µm long against 7.85 µm: 7 % longer for the same footprint.
  2. Make it circular. Set R = 2 µm and p = 0: the curvature jumps from 0 to 1/R at both ends, and the mode steps 100 nm outward where the straight guide meets the bend.
  3. Go back to Euler. At p = 1 the mode drifts out and back smoothly, leaning 140 nm out at the tightest radius of 1.07 µm. Below 2–3 µm a silicon strip starts to radiate, which this demo does not model.
