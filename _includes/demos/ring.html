Three things to try

  1. Reach critical coupling. At the 250 nm gap, raise the loss to 3.6 dB/cm, so that t = a: the CRITICAL pill lights up, the dip passes 40 dB and the through port goes dark, while the ring holds about 190 times the input power.
  2. Detune the laser. Slide the detuning to about 1 FWHM: the ring keeps only a fifth of its power and the through port refills. At the end of the slider, half an FSR away, the ring is dark and the light passes by.
  3. Drop a channel. Switch to add-drop and lower the loss to 0.3 dB/cm: on resonance the drop port takes almost all the light (t1 ≈ t2a), and the field leaves to the left along the upper bus.
