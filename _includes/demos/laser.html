Three things to try

  1. Cross threshold. Lower the current below 19.2 mA: the pill reads BELOW THRESHOLD and the cavity goes dark. At 50 mA the front facet gives 6.45 mW, the 6.5 mW of Worked Example 24.1.
  2. Coat the rear facet. Switch to the HR-coated rear: the mirror loss falls, the threshold drops to 13.9 mA and the front facet now gets 92 % of the light. The standing wave is almost complete near the rear mirror.
  3. Heat it, or let it heat itself. At 85 °C the threshold climbs to 52.3 mA (T0 = 60 K). Back at 25 °C, set Zth = 80 K/W, a laser on the buried oxide: the current scale grows to 600 mA and the output peaks at 63.8 mW per facet at 420 mA, then rolls over. A 600 µm cavity on a 55 °C heat sink at 100 mA is Worked Example 27.1: 20.2 mW out at 1.50 V, 130 mW of heat, 10.4 K hotter, threshold +19 %.
