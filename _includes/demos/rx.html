Three things to try

  1. Find the sensitivity. Lower the received power to −11.5 dBm: Q is just above 7.03 and the BER just below 10⁻¹². One step further, at −11.6 dBm, Q is 6.99 and the errors are too many: the sensitivity lies in between.
  2. Add FEC. Switch the target to 2.4 × 10⁻⁴: Q need only be 3.49, the sensitivity moves to −14.7 dBm and the margin grows from 8.9 to 12.0 dB.
  3. Try an APD. Switch to APD and sweep the gain: the sensitivity is best, −19.2 dBm, near M = 13. At M = 20 the multiplication noise has pushed it back to −18.9 dBm.
