Three things to try

  1. Image the spot. At the start the lens forms a 4.94 µm waist 426 µm behind it, as in Worked Example 3.2, and the fibre at 430 µm collects 100 %. The crests are flat at the source and at the image waist, and curved in between.
  2. Defocus. Move the fibre to d2 = 600 µm. It now meets a beam 18 µm wide with curved wavefronts, and the coupling falls to 26 %.
  3. Change fibre. Choose UHNA: the 4.94 µm spot puts only 48 % into its 2 µm mode. Set d1 = 175 µm for a 2.0 µm waist, then d2 = 233 µm to put the fibre on it: the coupling is back to 100 %.
