Three things to try

  1. Find Pπ. With the plain TiN heater, raise the power to 26 mW: the phase reaches 1.00 π, a 180° shift. Now change the length: ΔT falls from 13.7 K at 300 µm to 4.1 K at 1 mm, but the phase does not move.
  2. Isolate the heater. Switch to trenches: Pπ falls to 16.4 mW and the rise slows only to 21 µs. Switch to undercut: 4.5 mW, but 179 µs.
  3. Heat the waveguide itself. Switch to doped silicon: Pπ is 22.1 mW, and the step response starts at once, reaching 10 % in 0.04 µs instead of 1.4 µs.
