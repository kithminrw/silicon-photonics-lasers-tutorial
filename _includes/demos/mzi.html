Three things to try

  1. Build a switch. Set ΔL to 0: the arms are balanced and all the light leaves by the cross port. Raise the heater to 1.00π and it moves to the bar port; at 0.50π it splits 50/50.
  2. Set the FSR. ΔL = 100 µm gives fringes 5.75 nm apart, as in Worked Example 11.2. Shorten it to 90 µm for the 6.4 nm of an interleaver.
  3. Unbalance a splitter. Set the second splitter to 55 % and switch to the dB scale: the nulls fill in and the extinction falls to about 26 dB, as in Worked Example 11.1.

Check yourselfThe first splitter sends 45 % to the lower arm and the second 55 %. Which output still has deep nulls?
